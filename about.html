About Us

PowerWise is a website created for the COS30045 Data Visualisation unit. Our goal is to help Australian households understand how much energy their appliances use, so they can make better buying decisions.

Our Mission

To present clear, easy-to-understand information about appliance energy consumption in the Australian market.

Our Data

Our television story uses the Australian Government's Energy Rating registration data for TVs, published on data.gov.au. The charts were produced with KNIME.
